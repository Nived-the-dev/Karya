Kārya — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Kārya (कार्य) is an offline-first homework and habit organiser. The application is built to function without an account, advertisements, analytics, a server, or external artificial intelligence services.

Information stored on your device

When you use Kārya, the application stores tasks, subjects, descriptions, due dates, subtasks, priorities, habits, habit schedules, check-ins, and reminder preferences in its own local application storage (SQLite and device preferences). This information is used to present your task board, activity charts, progress and reminders.

Information we collect and share

The app is not designed to send your task or habit content to Kārya's developer or third parties. There is no account registration, third-party analytics, advertising SDK, online syncing or transmission to an AI service. Local notifications are scheduled through Android on the device. The app uses Flutter and standard local-storage and notification libraries.

Permissions

With your permission, Kārya can display local notifications for homework deadlines and habits. Permission is optional; if denied, tasks and habits still work. Android also uses a boot-completed receiver to restore scheduled reminders after the device reboots. The app does not request camera, contacts, GPS, storage access or internet permissions.

Android backup

This Android build sets backup and device-transfer extraction rules to exclude application data. Device vendors may implement operating-system data handling differently; the publisher must verify the shipped configuration during testing. Data stored on your device may be accessible through device-level administrator or system tooling outside the app.

Data retention and deletion

Information remains stored locally until you delete it. You can use Settings → Delete all app data to permanently remove saved records, or uninstall Kārya. There are no online accounts to delete.

Children and students

Kārya is a general-purpose educational productivity tool. It does not intentionally collect personal information through a website or server. The intended age groups must be accurately declared by the publisher in Google Play Console before release.

Changes

The publisher may update this policy to reflect app changes or new requirements. The public version should carry the latest effective date.
