**App name:** Kārya — Tasks & Habits

**Short description (under 80 characters):** Organise homework, track habits, and stay on top of your day—offline.

**Full description:**

Meet Kārya (कार्य), your cheerful study companion designed to help you spend less time organising and more time doing.

Keep your homework and assignments together, sort them into colourful subjects, set deadlines, create subtasks and tick them off. Build daily or weekly habits, view your completion history, and find your rhythm with a seven-day progress chart and monthly activity calendar.

Kārya includes a quick command assistant for simple actions like adding tasks, reviewing today's work, and checking your progress. It follows reliable commands—not a generative AI model.

Why Kārya?
- Bright, playful neo-brutalist design with light and dark modes
- Homework and tasks organised by subject, priority and due date
- Daily habits with custom weekday schedules
- Weekly graphs and a monthly progress heatmap based on your activity
- Optional local assignment and habit reminders
- Works offline without an account
- No ads, subscriptions or cloud AI required
